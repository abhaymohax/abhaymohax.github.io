Project Concept

AgriLink Procurement is a digital record system for merchants who purchase wheat, grains and vegetables directly from small farmers.

Problem

Small agricultural suppliers and local procurement businesses often depend on paper records, making it difficult to track purchase history, quantities, payments and quality.

Solution

The system creates a structured digital record at the moment produce is purchased. The merchant can later search the farmer, verify the transaction and monitor procurement totals.

Additional data fields added

  • Quality grade
  • Payment status
  • Collection location
  • Purchase rate per kg
  • Calculated total purchase value
  • Quality / inspection notes

Future scope

  • Farmer ID and Aadhaar-independent business identification
  • Digital weighing-machine integration
  • Photo of produce / quality certificate
  • Inventory after sorting and rejection
  • Transport and logistics tracking
  • Buyer/export order management
  • Real cloud database with staff login
